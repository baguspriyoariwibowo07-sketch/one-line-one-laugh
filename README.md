> My microwave beeps when the food is ready. It also beeps when I walk past, just to remind me it still exists.

- Fresh attention mechanism: the microwave beeps not to signal function, but to be noticed — an appliance seeking attention, not announcing readiness.
- Tight: a single 109-character line with no wasted words; setup and payoff land in one breath.
- Unexpected turn: the second beep reframes the first — what sounded like a feature becomes a cry for existence.
- Memorable: the personified, slightly needy microwave sticks in the mind and invites a knowing laugh of recognition.
